SYNTHETIC CLARIFICATION ROUND 2. Scripted replies from fictional participants for a workflow test; these are not human review approvals.
Luis: Send one payment reminder seven calendar days after the wait for the deposit begins, if payment is still outstanding then. It is a noninterrupting reminder: the same deposit wait stays active, and the Delivery work continues. It does not restart either clock and does not repeat. Once the reminder has been sent, that reminder action finishes; financial clearance still depends on receiving the deposit. Send the reminder to the external client.
Maya and Nora: The readiness deadline is fourteen calendar days from entry into readiness, after intake is complete. If readiness has not completed by then, interrupt the whole readiness stage, including any active Finance and Delivery work. The deadline is not fourteen days from the last reminder and not a timer only on the deposit task. The cancellation procedure begins instead of technical setup. A client withdrawal message received while readiness is active has the same interrupting effect. This model covers withdrawal and expiry during readiness only; cancellation after setup has begun is a separate process outside this scope.
Luis and Maya: On either readiness deadline or withdrawal, check whether a deposit was received. If one was received, Finance refunds that deposit; if none was received, skip the refund. After that check or refund, Maya releases any provisional delivery slot, records the cancellation, and the case ends as onboarding canceled. Releasing a nonexistent provisional hold is harmless. Any partial plan work stops, and no kickoff or handover happens on this cancellation path. This test assumes one full deposit, with no partial payments, disputes or refund failures.
